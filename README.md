
# Manufacturing Business Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

An end-to-end Business Analyst project focused on evaluating the operational and financial performance of a fictional manufacturing company.

The project investigates production efficiency, sales performance, inventory management, manufacturing costs, and profitability.

## Business Problem

Management wants to understand why production costs are increasing, inventory is accumulating, and profitability varies across products.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze production performance.
- Identify profitable and underperforming products.
- Evaluate inventory levels.
- Analyze sales and revenue trends.
- Identify cost inefficiencies.
- Provide data-driven business recommendations.

## Key Business Questions

1. Which products generate the highest revenue?
2. Which products have the highest profit margins?
3. What is the monthly production trend?
4. Are there inventory shortages or excess stock?
5. Which production areas have the highest costs?
6. What improvements can increase profitability?
